Who We Are

Making Movement Meaningful.

SPORTZEN is a physical education programme focused on young children. We use age-appropriate games and movement activities to support the development of fundamental movement skills, coordination, confidence and active habits.

Our approach centres on engaging physical experiences that encourage children to participate, practise skills and enjoy being active.

Our Programmes

Move With Purpose

Physical Education for Young Learners

Our physical education activities use age-appropriate games and structured movement experiences to help children practise essential physical skills.

Activities can be planned around movement development, coordination, balance, participation and the introduction of sports skills.

  • Fundamental movement skills
  • Balance and coordination
  • Agility and motor-skill development
  • Age-appropriate games and activities
01

Fundamental Movement Skills

Activities that give children opportunities to practise basic movement patterns, body control and movement awareness.

02

Balance & Coordination

Movement activities that encourage balance, coordination and controlled movement through age-appropriate physical experiences.

03

Agility & Motor Skills

Activities involving movement, direction changes, body awareness and the practice of essential motor skills.

04

Skating

Skating activities can form part of a programme planned around children's needs, ability levels and appropriate safety measures.

05

Fitness & Basic Athletics

Age-appropriate movement activities that introduce children to basic athletics, physical participation and active play.

06

Sports Skills & Activity Days

Opportunities to introduce sports skills and organise engaging sports events or activity days suited to the school's requirements.
